**Last reviewed: August 29, 2026**

SeedanceAI.dev is an independent AI video workspace. It brings supported Seedance and third-party video models into one model-aware generator that shows compatible controls and the product credit charge before submission.

We are not affiliated with or endorsed by ByteDance, Doubao, Dreamina, or the owners of other models listed on the site. Model names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

## How we review product claims

We separate three kinds of information:

1. **Available here:** controls and model routes that are implemented in the current product and accepted by its server-side allowlist.
2. **Provider information:** capabilities or limits documented by the relevant upstream provider.
3. **Editorial guidance:** practical recommendations based on the visible controls, pricing, and workflow, rather than a guarantee of output quality.

Fact-heavy guides show a last-reviewed date and link to their sources. When upstream documentation conflicts with the current generator, the generator's visible product controls define what is available on SeedanceAI.dev.

## Pricing method

Product credits are not the same as a provider's billing unit. We review provider cost, payment fees, failed-task behavior, and operating margin before setting product credit rates. The generator is the source of truth for the exact charge of a selected model, resolution, and duration. The [pricing page](/pricing) explains the current credit wallets.

We do not silently route a request to a higher-cost provider when the published product rate would not cover that route.

## Visual and output evidence

Images labeled as visual studies or concept studies are original direction references created for this site. They are not represented as output from a listed model. AI output remains variable; examples and prompt guidance do not guarantee that another generation will match them.

## Corrections and contact

Model availability, provider terms, and pricing can change. To report an error, request a correction, or ask a privacy question, email [hello@seedanceai.dev](mailto:hello@seedanceai.dev). Include the page URL and the specific statement you believe needs review.
