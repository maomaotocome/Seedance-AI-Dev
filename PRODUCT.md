# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Creators, marketers, filmmakers, and developers arriving with practical intent: they want to compare current AI video models, understand the real controls and cost, and generate a usable clip without first learning provider-specific APIs. Public pages are English-first with complete Chinese parity.

## Product Purpose

Seedance AI is an independent multi-model AI video workspace. It turns search and comparison intent into a transparent creation workflow: choose a supported model and input mode, see the available limits and exact site credit charge, generate, then inspect and download the result. Success means visitors can decide whether the product fits their shot and begin a valid generation from the first viewport.

## Brand Personality

Direct, cinematic, and technically honest. The experience should feel like a dependable director's console: confident enough for professional creative work, calm enough to understand quickly, and specific enough to avoid generic AI-tool aesthetics.

## Anti-references

- Generic neon-purple, blue, or green AI gradients and glow.
- Decorative 3D, Spline, or WebGL scenes in the critical path.
- Centered marketing copy that pushes the working generator below the fold.
- Hype-heavy model fan pages, unsupported quality claims, and artificial scarcity.
- Excessive pills, nested cards, dashboard clutter, or empty preview areas that dominate the workflow.
- Third-party demo media presented as product output.

## Design Principles

1. Product before prose: let the working generator establish value in the first viewport.
2. Proof before superlatives: show real controls, real limits, sources, and output provenance.
3. Cost before commitment: disclose the exact site credit charge before generation.
4. Progressive precision: reveal only the controls supported by the selected model and workflow.
5. One useful intent per page: every model page must add unique specifications, use cases, prompts, and comparisons rather than swapping names.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast for public surfaces. All generator controls need semantic labels, keyboard access, visible focus, non-color status cues, and clear error recovery. Respect reduced-motion preferences, reserve media dimensions to prevent layout shift, and keep the prompt workflow immediately accessible on mobile. English and Chinese public content, metadata, navigation, and structured data must remain equivalent in meaning.
