# Seedance AI design context

## Audience

Creators, marketing teams, filmmakers, and developers arriving from Google with practical intent around Seedance 2.5, Seedance 2.0, free access, prompts, pricing, comparisons, and API integration. Current GSC evidence is strongest on the homepage, English-speaking markets, desktop, and free/brand query clusters.

## Brand personality

Direct, cinematic, technically honest, useful, and independent. The product should feel like a dependable creative instrument, not a hype-heavy model fan page.

## Aesthetic

Director's Desk: projection-room black, warm studio paper, signal orange, timecode amber, and restrained sage. Instrument Sans leads; Newsreader is a sparing editorial accent. The working generator and real output evidence take priority over decorative visuals.

October 13 user feedback: preserve the original bright landing page, with a large left title and separate right-hand film. Keep the working generator directly after this composition on desktop, and directly after concise copy on mobile. Do not repeat the rejected all-dark, form-led landing-page direction.

## What to avoid

- Generic purple/blue/green AI gradients and glow.
- Spline or WebGL decoration in the critical rendering path.
- Centered marketing copy followed by a generator below the fold.
- Excessive pill-shaped containers and nested cards.
- Unverified model, pricing, privacy, commercial-rights, or unlimited claims.
- Reused third-party demo media presented as product output.

## Principles

1. Product before prose.
2. Proof before superlatives.
3. Cost and limits before the generate action.
4. English-first with Chinese metadata, navigation, content, and structured-data parity.
5. One search intent per useful page; no thin keyword variants.
